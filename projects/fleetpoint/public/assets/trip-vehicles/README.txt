Map vehicle model

car.glb is IoTility's own model (no third-party licence): a pearl white crossover with dark
glass, black cladding, smoked LED headlights and a full-width red tail light bar. Every
vehicle is drawn with it on trip replay and on the vehicle detail live map
(shared/maps/vehicle-model.ts).

It is generated by scripts/fleet-car/build.mjs, in metres, facing +Z with Y up. To change
it, edit the script and rebuild:
  node scripts/fleet-car/build.mjs projects/fleetpoint/public/assets/trip-vehicles/car.glb
